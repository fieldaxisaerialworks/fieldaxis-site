Fieldaxis Aerialworks

A West Michigan aerial-services business being developed for property documentation, site progress imagery, and related small-UAS applications.

Commercial flight operations will begin following completion of FAA Part 107 certification.

Services

Aerial Documentation

High-resolution aerial imagery for documenting properties, structures, sites, and changing conditions from useful overhead and oblique perspectives.

Property & Roof Documentation

Systematic exterior and roof imagery intended to provide clear visual records of hard-to-access areas and visible conditions.

Site Progress Documentation

Repeatable aerial imagery for documenting construction, property improvement, and other site changes over time.

Service Area

Fieldaxis Aerialworks is based in the Grand Rapids area and is being developed to serve clients throughout West Michigan.

Project availability will depend on location, airspace, operating conditions, and applicable FAA requirements.

About

Fieldaxis Aerialworks LLC is an independent Michigan business focused on practical small-UAS applications for property, construction, and site documentation.

Fieldaxis is currently in its pre-launch stage while FAA Part 107 certification and operational capabilities are being completed.
